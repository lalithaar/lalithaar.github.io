# Design for accessibility before you design for coolness, and your future self will thank you

It completely dismantles the trap of building for the aesthetic portfolio or the flashy demo reel instead of the actual human being—including your tired future self—who has to maintain, debug, or use the thing at 3 AM.

Accessibility isn’t a checklist you tack on at the end; it’s the foundation that keeps a system from collapsing into an unusable monument to your past self's ego.
