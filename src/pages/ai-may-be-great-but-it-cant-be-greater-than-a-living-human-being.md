**AI may be great, but it can't be greater than a living human being.** This is something that's been the opposite of how people and people building AI speak about it and it's relation with humans. Electricity is incredible, art is incredible, replace that word with any of the wonderful inventions. 

But most of these discussions confuse the impact/skills/career of a person with their worth.

"what value do you have when there is AI which can do it" 
"AI is gonna replace you"
"AI won't replace you but people using AI will"

- All of these conflate the worth of a person, something that's inherent, and is never something that can be changed by what you do or don't, with what impact their work could have, where they can contribute. 
  
[It's okay to hold both the good or bad aspects of it, and still know where it's boundaries are.](/honoring-what-a-system-holds-and-seeing-what-it-leaves-out)

> "Art may be great, but it cannot be greater than a living human being. I am still greater than those paintings that are hung on that wall. Because I am a living being and I will continue to live."
>
> — Sung Duk-mi, an art museum curator (Her Private Life kdrama)
