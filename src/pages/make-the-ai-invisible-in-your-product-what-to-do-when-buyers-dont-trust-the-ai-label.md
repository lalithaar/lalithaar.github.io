# Make the AI invisible in your product: what to do when buyers don't trust the AI label

I have been talking to some folks from AI ecosystem and saw some interesting applications, but here is the thing with AI powered label: people see AI and assume it is an AI wrapper or just another AI slop, but there are lots of wonderful products where AI is the least powerful thing in what makes the product useful. Because what matters is the workflows, the functions, and the loops around it. 

People are skeptical and also tired of seeing AI wrappers because, honestly, there are quite a bit of wrappers too - whether it was a wrapper to begin with, or just a product whose value proposition got absorbed because the AI vendor released that feature natively. That is one thing.

If it's say an AI landing page generator, don't say hey *"it's an AI powered next generation landing page generator"*.

What does it have underneath? The constraints, rules, boundaries you set around the API fetch, immediate preview for someone without a tech knowledge.

Highlight the benefits, for real. Say **"Helps you spin up landing pages faster, so you focus on building a great product instead of tweaking CSS"**

Let's think of a CRM which can now use AI to enrich the contacts - because at the end of the day they have some data and they have some missing fields, which you are using the AI web search to fill, and AI helps you bridge that gap. It's a real problem with a brilliant solution you created, nice. But take some credit, please?

Say it helps you enrich client data instead of manual search and wrangle, not *"AI Powered CRM"*

You may ask "But Lalitha - isn't AI so wonderful? and we tackled a solution to an annoying problem, and solved it with AI"

AI is great, yes. Powerful. I use AI. But like I said, AI is the least impressive thing about the product usually. But if you are using the AI as the main highlight, maybe it's time to stop doing that. I am proud of the solution you cooked up. 

But what the customer cares of is, how does it help me? (brutal I know, I would love to talk of how I elegantly set up SSR for SEO critical pages and SPA for others too ://)

More proofs?

Let's take Whatsapp, WhatsApp doesn't market its language with 'we use this system, these complex technology networks, and everything to make the sender and receiver get the responses immediately.'

They just say, 'This is a real-time chat' because that is the benefit. The thing audience cares about.

SQLite doesn't say, "Hey, this is how the B-tree re-indexes children, blah blah blah," but it just says, "Hey, this is how we produce an index that can help you save costs with repeated, most frequent fetches." 

Basically, list how it helps "Use advanced filters to find exactly what you need", not the 28 if else we use to filter data. 

**One single question you can use to test which it is saying:** Is it impressive to a fellow engineer to understand how it works, or does it feel like relief to a headache for the customer?
