# Found a Baby Bird in Tamil Nadu? Read This First

You have/see a tiny bird you think needs rescue?

> [!CAUTION]
> **Do NOT list:**
> - Do NOT feed water or milk or ANY liquid.
> - Do NOT flip it on it's back to check for injuries, it doesn't have the capacity to breathe like that and will suffocate.
> - Do NOT poke or touch or pet it or let children touch it, it is NOT a pet and it's survival is more important than petting or showcasing because it's cute.

 
Here's what to do:
**Don't panic.** You got this. This guide will help you out. 

Is it atleast **slightly feathered**? Doesn't look exactly as grown as an adult or baby but still tiny. 
- See if it's injured, if not - don't touch it, if you have already taken it in your hands, put it back where you found it. 
- Most of the time, the parents are nearby and will come back to feed it. If you see a parent bird, leave the area and let them do their job. 
- If it's in a dangerous place, like a road or a place where it can be easily attacked by cats or dogs, you can move it to a nearby bush or tree. But don't take it home. The parents will find it.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you see AI or usual blogs saying something else, I have discussed why below, we can worry of it later. I have faced this exact doubt too. Saving the bird is the priority over advices that mimic the textbook but isn't possible in real life in Tamilnadu, yeah. 

If it's a baby bird that is **not feathered** and/or injured


**Now, the actuals of how exactly do I help it:**
- take it slowly and place it in a cloth (make sure it doesn't have holes or open threads, a old cotton cloth that's clean and not torn is enough)
- If it felt cold to hand when picking it up, place in your palm instead for it to warm. DON'T touch or press anywhere, it cannot maintain it's temperature yet and it needs alot of warmth which your hand naturally provides.
- **Do NOT feed it water** or milk first. A bird this young doesn't have the power needed to intake a liquid properly. Don't do it with syringe. It could go to air pipe instead of food pipe and it can't just cough it up like humans. 
- See if you have unseasoned (no salt, or spice or anything close to cooking other than pure boiled) boiled egg - if so, take just a tiny bit (like 1 ml / 2 gms max. yes, that tiny) and mix it with water into a paste - it should not be liquid or solid fully, but rather think of tomato sauce texture. Both egg whites and yolk work, yolk is a bit easier to make it into a paste.
-  Don't feed it with synringe, wash your hands (make sure not to use any scented soap or too chemical/beauty one). If used soap, wash the second time without a soap and dry your hands and use your index finger tip to take tiny bit (like think a size of a rice, don't take big chunks - the 2gms mix would come for atleast 6-7 takes in this quantity and it won't eat all of it). If you have an ink piller with soft end, you can use that like how you would use a tooth picker - don't actually suck food into it, but use it to take just a bit like spoon - this is easier than finger. 
- It will gap wide and it's expected (and a good sign), but if it ate just 2-3 rice like amount and stopped gaping, STOP feeding. It's stomach is small enough that this is enough.
- **If you do not have boiled egg and need to boil it and it takes ~15 minutes**, one and ONLY then - take just few drops of milk (again it's risky, but this is purely so it doesn't go unconciousness or dehydrate)
- Use a tiny box with enough air for it to breathe, but not so much that it's cold - **It's temperature should be as warm as possible** like think of human fever range 100.4°F to 104°F is it's normal body temperature. 
- A  plastic box with holes  is better as it doesn't transfer the cold from the floor. Inside plastic box, keep the soft clothes stacked and place it inside. Keep it in a dark place and that would mean under the bed, room with curtains closed, make syre it has enough air to breathe (DO NOT keep it near the fan, just usual breathing space is enough, fan will be cold and potentially fatal to a tiny bird )
- You cannot reliably detect what bird it is at this point, but check if it has a pouch like thing under it's face, neck. It's called a crop. If it has one, it should look a bit full (don't expect it to look or puff like a baloon) now....
