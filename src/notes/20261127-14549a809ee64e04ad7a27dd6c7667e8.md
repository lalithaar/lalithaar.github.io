What I filter by when choosing softwares these days:
- No AI, the 'AI' features for the sake of the app pretending to be all *modernised*, features that no one asked for. DON'T fix what isn't broken.
- There are truly amazing applications of AI where it solves a really annoying problem well, and in almost every app that does it well - the AI is invisible. Instead of marketing it as the ultimate AI optimised paradigm changing app, just talk about the problem it solves and never use the word AI in copy. I don't think we ever suddenly renamed every app's copy to say *the frontend is now built with react instead of JSX*, *we updated npm from 1.12.2 to 1.12.13 in the copy*. Stop doing it with AI please.
- No design that's added to match a particular trend (purple gradients, webgl animation, the noir grainy grey, terminal), the app has a stance on design and what it needs to have and doesn't just chase every trend.
- Clear data collection, retention, privacy, customer stance.

In a nutshell, do the character building part well instead of a hollow shell slapping every trope that's in trend.
